HERO Helmets: Bio-Composite Safety Helmet Innovation 
Team Leader Funded by Kemdikbudristek (PKM-KC Scheme)  
• Developed an industrial safety helmet using water hyacinth fiber biocomposites to 
promote sustainability and cost-efficiency. 
    • Conducted mechanical testing (Impact Resistance and Tensile Strength tests) to ensure 
    compliance with safety standards (SNI/ASTM). 
    • Designed the helmet prototype and optimized the manufacturing process using vacuum 
    infusion (atau metode lain yang Anda pakai) technique. 
    • Analyzed material properties to determine the optimal fiber-to-matrix ratio for 
    maximum durability
