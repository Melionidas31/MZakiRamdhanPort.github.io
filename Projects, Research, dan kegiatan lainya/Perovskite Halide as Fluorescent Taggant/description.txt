Feasibility Study: Perovskite Halide as Fluorescent Taggant 
Researcher 
    • Authored a feasibility study on utilizing Perovskite Halide materials as fluorescent 
    taggants for advanced Line Tracer applications in Oil & Gas operations. 
    • Analyzed the optical properties and stability of the material to detect leaks or trace flow 
    paths in pipeline systems. 
    • Evaluated the economic and technical viability of implementing this nanomaterial 
    technology compared to conventional tracing methods.
