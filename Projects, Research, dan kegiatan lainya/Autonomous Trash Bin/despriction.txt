Autonomous Trash Bin: Line Follower Based Automation  
Capstone Project Leader 
    • Led the end-to-end development of a mechatronic waste management robot, overseeing 
    mechanical design, circuit integration, and sensor programming. 
    • Engineered an automated navigation system using IR sensors (Line Follower) and 
    microcontroller logic to execute precise waste collection routes. 
    • Designed the touchless actuation mechanism utilizing Ultrasonic sensors and Servo 
    motors, enabling automatic lid operation for improved hygiene. 
    • Conducted performance testing on sensor sensitivity and motor capability, optimizing 
    the robot’s response time and load-carrying efficiency.
