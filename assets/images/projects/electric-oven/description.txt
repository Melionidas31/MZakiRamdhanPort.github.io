Prototype Analysis of Low-Cost Electric Oven 
Heat Transfer Course Project 
    • Designed and fabricated a functional prototype oven utilizing upcycled heating elements 
    (from rice cooker) and auxiliary incandescent lighting. 
    • Engineered a composite insulation system using aluminum foil, cardboard, and 
    polystyrene (styrofoam) to minimize heat loss via conduction and radiation. 
    • Analyzed the heating rate and thermal retention of the chamber to evaluate the 
    efficiency of the insulation materials.
